Harmony Editor: web UI mockups

The Home Assistant add-on editor. Desktop frames are 1240 px wide and scroll sideways if your window is narrower. Phone frames are 400 px. Colours follow your system theme; each frame uses the same tokens the real app will ship.

1 · Overview, with the draft bar

The draft bar stays at the top of every screen. It shows whether the draft differs from what is applied, whether it is valid, and how full the hub is. It has two actions: Discard draft and Review & apply.

3 · Add device: the power test step

Archive codes are grouped into distinct power codes, most common first. Each try fires one code. The answer moves the loop on, and "Yes" narrows the model list to the codesets that use that code. Sending is rate-limited, and the button shows a countdown while it waits.

You pick the devices and their inputs, then the start sequence is built for you. The power and input steps are generated and locked, and your own steps follow. The "Who handles it" pickers fill the activity's key layer, and a key you override on the Hard keys screen wins.

A schematic of the Elite's 35 physical keys. Choose a layer (Default, or one activity), then click a key or Tab through them. Filled keys are overridden in this layer, light keys inherit from Default, and dashed keys do nothing.

6 · Remote page editor

The canvas is the compiler's decoded page, drawn at 2× in the remote's own colours. Cells show a faint grid. You can drag to move, drag the corner handles to resize, or use the keyboard: arrows move, Shift+arrows resize. The ghost cell marks where "Add widget" puts the new one. The byte and widget meters update as you type.
